# The Vihaan Scale (°V)
**Inventor:** Vihaan S.  
**Status:** Protected & Copyrighted  

### Core Anchor Points:
- **0°V** = Freezing point of water (0°C / 32°F)
- **100°V** = Average human body temperature (37°C / 98.6°F)
- **Boiling Point:** ~270°V (100°C)

### The Conversion Formula:
Vihaan Scale (°V) = (Celsius / 37) * 100

### Why the Vihaan Scale?
Designed for everyday weather, biology, and medical sciences. By anchoring 0°V to freezing and 100°V to human body temperature, all human-centric environments and fevers fit neatly into a 0-to-100 scale, eliminating negative numbers for biological use.
